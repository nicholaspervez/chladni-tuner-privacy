Klangfigur Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy applies to Klangfigur for Android and iPhone. Klangfigur is designed to work offline and keep its app data on your device.

Microphone

Klangfigur requests microphone access so it can detect the pitch of an instrument. Microphone audio is analysed in real time in memory on your device. The app does not record, save, transmit or share microphone audio.

If microphone permission is declined, live pitch detection is unavailable. You can withdraw microphone permission at any time in your device settings.

Information stored on the device

Display, microphone-sensitivity and other tuner preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Klangfigur does not send these preferences to Red Sun Rising LTD or to third parties.

You can remove the app's stored data by uninstalling Klangfigur or, on Android, by clearing its storage. Copies included in an operating-system device backup are controlled through your Apple or Google backup settings.

Data collection and sharing

Klangfigur does not collect or share personal information, microphone audio, identifiers, diagnostics, usage analytics, advertising data or location data. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send app data over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the changed version is released.
