Chladni Tuner Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

Chladni Tuner is designed to work offline and to keep its data on your device.

Microphone

Chladni Tuner requests microphone access so it can detect the pitch of an instrument. Microphone audio is analysed in real time in memory. The app does not record, save, transmit or share microphone audio.

If microphone permission is declined, live pitch detection is unavailable. Reference-note playback remains available in supported tuner modes.

Information stored on the device

If you create a custom tuning, its name and note list are stored in the app's private storage on your device. This information is not sent off the device. You can delete a custom tuning in the app, or remove all app data by uninstalling Chladni Tuner or clearing its storage in Android settings.

Android cloud backup is disabled for Chladni Tuner.

Data collection and sharing

Chladni Tuner does not collect or share personal information, microphone audio, identifiers, diagnostics, usage analytics, advertising data or location data. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not request internet access.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on Chladni Tuner's Google Play listing.
